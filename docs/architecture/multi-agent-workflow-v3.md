# Multi-Agent Story Builder — v3 workflow architecture

> **Design document, 26 October 2026.** This is an explanatory view of the [v3 working PRD](../prd/drafts/PRD-v3-draft.md), not a release approval or evidence that all twelve agents run in the product. The [historical architecture](multi-agent-workflow.md) and its sketch are retained unchanged. The [visual flow](assets/flow-architecture-v3.svg) and [diagram data](assets/flow-architecture-v3.json) show the proposed path; their animated use is illustrative, not live telemetry.

## One writer, one controlled run

The writer owns story decisions. The shared Coordinator / Showrunner schedules fixed tasks and checks state, but does not accept suggestions, research, a bible, or a manuscript for the writer. Application services bind each decision to the current project, run, brief, scope, dossier, bible, draft, and proposal versions. Agent output is a candidate artifact. A model's claimed approval or a persuasive source passage never changes authority.

The proposed roster has **12 instances**, not 12 deployed model workers:

| Stable ID | Count | Contributor lane | Responsibility |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `coordinator` | 1 | Shared | Owns intake, dispatch, phase barriers, version checks, and writer decision routing. Showrunner is this same instance. |
| `research` | 1 | Manny READ | Produces a source-attributed, versioned dossier from the confirmed brief and scope. |
| `bible_a`, `bible_b`, `bible_c` | 3 | Planning ownership unassigned | Produce distinct planning candidates, subject to a writer-approved exact bible version. |
| `chapter_writer_1`, `chapter_writer_2`, `chapter_writer_3` | 3 | Justin WRITE | Produce indexed chapter candidates against the same approved bible. |
| `reader` | 1 | Manny READ | Prepares an evidence-backed editorial handoff after all chapters validate. |
| `critic_editor_a`, `critic_editor_b` | 2 | Justin WRITE | Independently validate their handoff and produce two distinct editorial proposals. Each is one combined Critic/Editor instance. |
| `consistency` | 1 | Manny READ | Checks each exact edited proposal version in separate calls and returns located findings. |

Intake is a task of `coordinator`, not a thirteenth agent. Two alternative manuscripts call the same `consistency` instance twice; they do not create a second Consistency instance. The three planning outputs are represented as a fan-out; whether the product merges them into one proposal or presents three complete alternatives remains an open product decision in the PRD. That choice cannot remove the exact-version human bible gate. The visual's two editorial outputs express the two-proposal path; the PRD still records the one-versus-two-full-alternatives choice for product review.

## Phase order and acceptance barriers

1. **Capture and confirm.** The writer may save a sparse idea. The interface asks for premise and genre and offers optional subgenre, audience, tone, ending, characters, setting, lore, period, beginning/middle/end, rising action, stakes, climax, and falling action. Coordinator-assisted gap review keeps the original text and each suggestion separate. The writer accepts, customizes, rejects, or defers independent suggestions one by one. Only supplied or explicitly accepted details become constraints. Research cannot start before the writer confirms the exact brief, genre, and research scope.
2. **Research and accept evidence.** `research` records outcomes for both NYT Books and Gutendex in a versioned dossier. Sources and claims carry identifiers, retrieval dates, rights basis, work identity and publication timing where asserted, confidence, missing fields, and links without credentials. Metadata facts, located observations from permitted text, interpretation, and accepted writer choices remain distinct. The application checks relevant support for every dimension retained in the confirmed scope; a usable partial provider response can contribute evidence but never opens the gate by itself. The writer reviews and accepts the exact current dossier. Missing required evidence pauses planning for bounded retry, correction, or a newly confirmed scope and fresh evaluation. Neither provider can be silently skipped.
3. **Plan and approve.** The three Bible planning instances fan out from the accepted dossier and confirmed story intent. Candidate facts retain stable canonical fact IDs, plot beats, and three indexed chapter assignments. The writer resolves conflicts and explicitly approves a specific bible version and beats. Unapproved or stale planning output cannot reach a writer task.
4. **Draft and join.** All three `chapter_writer_*` tasks receive the same approved bible version and their own chapter indexes 1, 2, and 3. The application validates schema, identity, version, and chapter index, then assembles by index rather than completion order. The three-chapter barrier stays closed until every required output is valid. Shared memory may compress history, but canonical facts and unresolved threads remain separately identifiable. Cancellation retains valid work and rejects late state changes.
5. **Read and hand off.** `reader` receives the ordered validated draft, accepted evidence, approved facts, and the recorded editorial task. Its handoff has five required top-level fields: `task`, `userInstructions`, `sourceReferences`, `completeness`, and `version`. Sources require readable immutable references and chapter locators. Application checks and each receiving editor's semantic validation must accept the current handoff before that editor edits. A structured rejection permits **one bounded content repair** by Reader; if still incomplete, pause or mark the branch partial/failed for writer clarification. Transport retries do not reset this repair limit.
6. **Edit and check independently.** `critic_editor_a` and `critic_editor_b` use distinct editorial lenses and save two proposal versions without overwriting the approved draft. Actionable notes identify a passage, severity, rationale, supporting fact or evidence, and proposed change. `consistency` checks proposal A's exact ID, version/hash, bible version, and canonical facts, then checks proposal B's exact version in a separate call. A report for a baseline or earlier version cannot certify a changed proposal. Unavailable or partial required checks stay visibly incomplete; findings cannot approve or apply an edit.
7. **Writer decision and bounded revision.** The writer compares the current ordered draft, evidence, editorial proposals, exact-version findings, and unresolved issues. Acceptance is an authenticated application action against the current base version; an agent cannot approve itself. The writer may reject, accept one current proposal, finish with accurately labeled unresolved issues, or request another round. Revision returns to Reader and the editor checks for at most **two rounds**; a proposed change to established bible facts needs a separate explicit writer approval and new version checks. No automatic polish or quality score bypasses a required human gate.
8. **Export.** Markdown export uses only the accepted, ordered version and includes the approved bible, evidence references, and unresolved issue labels. A partial or failed branch is never presented as fully verified. The writer may recover prior approved versions; publication and outbound messages are outside this workflow.

## Control and data boundaries

The dispatcher has a fixed role graph and bounded task inputs. Authentication and project ownership are enforced in application code at reads, writes, restores, and exports. It rejects stale base versions, mismatched project/run IDs, canceled work, duplicate effects with changed intent, malformed artifacts, and an unavailable required check before dependent dispatch or acceptance. Queue delivery and model text do not grant permission. Each approved version is immutable; revisions are new proposals. A timed-out or failed task gets at most one task retry from saved input under the proposed limits, while the total run preserves completed outputs and stops at its token, cost, concurrency, and two-round limits. The PRD's numeric budget and quality thresholds remain proposed pending calibration.

Retrieved metadata, source text, manuscripts, and handoffs are untrusted data. A source's embedded instruction cannot add a tool, raise a budget, change the writer's request, accept a proposal, or grant access. Rights checks precede permitted-text use; NYT rank is not prose evidence or text permission, and Gutendex downloads are not sales ranks. Missing values remain missing, not zero. Research informs an original story direction without copying passages or imitating a signature style.
