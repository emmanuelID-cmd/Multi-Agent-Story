Owner: Manny

## Prerequisites

- Phase 2B.2 provides verified, provenance-bearing vocabulary choices.

## Steps

1. Offer zero to three verified options for a grounded intake choice; fewer than three are valid.
2. Present one clear question and a **Your answer** path when no defensible choice is available.
3. Preserve the writer's original/custom answer as `UNMAPPED`; never replace it with a guessed authority label.
4. Use authoritative choices only for fields supported by the writer's intent. Do not add arbitrary authority vocabulary to lore, tone, or point of view.
5. Keep existing saved v1/v2 inputs readable and preserve their original content.

## Acceptance checklist

- [x] A field can show zero through three verified options, including fewer than three.
- [x] A question and **Your answer** route remain available when no defensible option exists.
- [x] Original/custom answers survive as `UNMAPPED`, unchanged and visible for review.
- [x] Lore, tone and point of view do not receive unsupported authority choices.
- [x] Existing v1/v2 saved intake data remains compatible.

## Out of scope

- Replacing or normalizing user-authored answers to a closest match.
- Inventing choices or expanding authority vocabulary into unsupported fields.
- Any changes to Justin-owned Bible planning or WRITE behavior.
